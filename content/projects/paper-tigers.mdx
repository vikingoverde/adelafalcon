## Concept

Paper Tigers started as a margin doodle: a tiger, drawn in one acid-green line, sitting on top of a photo of a pink coat. The collection tries to keep that tension — something fierce drawn very lightly.

Every garment is cut from a flat paper pattern that is never "corrected" into a smooth curve. The kinks stay in. Hems are left raw and pressed flat so they read like torn paper.

<Note>fierce, but drawn lightly</Note>

## Colour & Muse

Blush, plum and milk, with one exception: a fluorescent green saddle stitch that appears on every look, always in the same place on the left shoulder.
