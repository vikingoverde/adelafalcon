## Concept

A forecast is a pattern you can wear. I collected a week of hourly weather readings for an imaginary coastal town and translated them, hour by hour, into rows of knitting.

<Note>tuesday = drizzle = drop stitch</Note>

## Colour & Muse

Wet-pavement blues and a single butter-yellow row for every hour of sunshine. The muse was the shipping forecast — calm voices reading out storms.

<Palette />

<Muse />

## Process

Swatching took longer than designing. Each weather state got its own stitch, tested for drape and warmth before it was allowed into the system.

<Process />

## Outcomes

- Seven one-off jumpers, one per day of the week.
- A small generative tool that turns any week of weather into a knit chart (placeholder).
- Shortlisted for a student knit prize (placeholder).
