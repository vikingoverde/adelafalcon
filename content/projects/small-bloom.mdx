## Concept

Small shops are made by hand, so their identity should be too. The mark is a flower drawn in one continuous line — staff learn to draw it in about ten minutes, and every version is correct.

<Note>the logo is a verb — you draw it</Note>

## Colour & Muse

Butter, mustard and a leaf green, borrowed from the bucket of tulips that was always by the door.

<Palette />

<Muse />

## Process

Dozens of drawn flowers, scanned and compared, until the rules were simple enough to teach: start at the stem, five petals, never lift the pen.

<Process />

## Outcomes

- Identity system, wrapping paper and stamp set.
- A one-page drawing guide for the shop team.
- Bags turned into a small collectable series (placeholder).
