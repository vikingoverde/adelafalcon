## Concept

Rosalía is one of the most influential Spanish artists working today, known for her experimental character, her fusion of genres and her references to many cultures. That complexity asked for a physical medium, so I designed and made a book-object about her and her aesthetic universe.

<Note>The reader does not simply observe Rosalía, but journeys through her world</Note>

## Structure

- Preludio — her musical side.
- Interludio — her creative side.
- Coda — her personal side: her family, her friends and the pillars that define her.

The sections unfold through a visual narrative that reveals her gradually, from the most superficial layer to the most profound.

## Research

The project took shape through an interview with Viktor Hammarberg, art director and graphic designer of the Motomami album, whose packaging won a Latin Grammy. His insights opened up the intentions behind each of her works.

## Typography

The book is built from lyrics of her own songs, which she sees as a form of self-portrait. They are set in a custom-made typeface instead of any existing font, to keep the voice close and personal, like hers.
